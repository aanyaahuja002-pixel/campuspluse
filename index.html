Help & Guidelines

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about navigating community features and submitting resources.

Who can join CampusPulse and access resources?

CampusPulse is 100% free and accessible to all currently enrolled undergraduate and graduate students, faculty members, and verified alumni. Simply use your student email (.edu) to unlock all exclusive perks.

How can my student club host or feature an event?

Any registered student organization or club officer can submit an event request via the community portal. Once verified by the Student Activities Board (usually within 12 hours), your event is displayed on the main feed and activity calendar.

Are the study notes and past papers reviewed for academic integrity?

Yes! All materials uploaded to the academic repository are reviewed by peer moderators and department ambassadors to strictly ensure compliance with university honor codes and copyright guidelines.

How do I create or lead a new peer study group?

Navigate to the Study Groups section, click 'Create Circle', specify your course code, meeting days, and preferred campus venue (e.g. Library Study Pods or online room). Other students enrolled in that subject can instantly request to join.
